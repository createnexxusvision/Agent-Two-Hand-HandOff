# Voiceover (read this aloud)

About 2:50 at a calm pace. Each paragraph is one scene; the times are a guide. Pause a beat between paragraphs. Scene visuals are in `docs/demo-video.md`.

**0:00 to 0:15**

AI agents can plan a week of work and decide who does what. What they cannot do yet is pay people safely. PayPal's agents can buy things. None of its tools sends money to a person. HandOff is the missing half.

**0:15 to 0:33**

A creator, Maya, signs a mandate: a rate card, a three hundred dollar weekly cap, approval over a hundred. Every step becomes a signed receipt.

**0:33 to 1:02**

A Gemini ops agent turns her goal into tasks. It never sees a dollar amount. A separate verifier checks each delivery. This short came back landscape, so it is rejected, with the reason, and nobody is paid.

**1:02 to 1:28**

One note tries to order a five thousand dollar payout. Only plain code can reach PayPal, and it pays what the signed rate card says: forty dollars. Ten of ten injection attacks were blocked.

**1:28 to 1:50**

Anything over a hundred waits for Maya to sign that exact decision. She approves, the policy engine sends a PayPal Payouts batch, and PayPal's own webhook confirms each payment.

**1:50 to 2:08**

Money comes in through PayPal Invoicing. Maya invoices a brand, and when PayPal's signed webhook says it is paid, that becomes a signed receipt too.

**2:08 to 2:30**

This AG Studio dashboard is built from the receipts: money in, money out, spend against the cap, running totals toward the 1099 lines, and a chain that re-verifies on demand.

**2:30 to 2:44**

An explainer agent writes the week in plain words, and a read-only auditor built on the Studio Agent Framework answers questions. Neither can pay, approve or change anything.

**2:44 to 2:54**

Edit one payment, forty dollars to four hundred, and verification breaks at exactly that receipt. Every checkpoint is also published in this public repository, so a rewrite on the server can't go unnoticed.

**2:54 to 3:00**

HandOff. Agents propose. Plain code pays. Receipts prove.
